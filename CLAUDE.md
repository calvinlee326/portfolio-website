## Default aesthetic

Near-monochrome ground with exactly one saturated accent held under 5%
coverage and given a single job; heavy or ultralight neo-grotesque display
in a large jump (2.5–6x) straight to body text with no mid-tier heading;
hierarchy from weight, value-shift, and negative space, never from borders
or added colors; full-bleed photography or product renders that bleed off
the frame instead of sitting in cards; restrained, confident, quietly
staged mood — never decorative, never busy.
